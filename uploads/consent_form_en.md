## 1. Invitation

You are invited to take part in a short feasibility study. Taking part is voluntary. Refusing will not affect any care you receive.

## 2. What we are testing

VYTRA is a phone application for trained health workers. It photographs the **inner surface of the lower eyelid** and the **white of the eye**, then estimates a **risk class** (low, moderate, high, or unable to assess) for anemia and for jaundice.

It is **not** a medical diagnosis. It does **not** measure hemoglobin or bilirubin. It is **not** a certified medical device.

## 3. What will happen if you agree

1. A trained operator will explain the steps and answer questions.
2. You (or your guardian) will sign this form.
3. The operator will photograph a white sheet of paper, then your everted lower lid (you look up; the lid is gently pulled down), then the outer white of your open eye.
4. Each part may be photographed up to three times.
5. The operator may also record a skin-tone category and the lighting in the room, and — only if you already have one from the last 48 hours — a clinic hemoglobin or bilirubin value, or a pulse-oximeter reading, on **this paper**, not in the app.
6. The visit lasts about ten minutes.

## 4. What is stored, and for how long

**On the phone**

- Stored: risk class, time, phone model, skin-tone category, lighting, technical quality numbers.
- **Not** stored: your name, your photograph, your location, your phone number.
- Photographs are analysed in memory and discarded.
- If a referral PDF is made, a name you choose may be written **on the PDF only** and is not kept in the app.
- Phone records become **eligible for deletion 30 days** after capture and are removed the next time the app cleans up. Uninstalling the app deletes the phone copy immediately.

**On the study server (only if the phone later has internet)**

- An anonymised copy of the same fields may be sent.
- No name and no photograph are sent.
- Server copies follow the study’s own deletion schedule (no later than 90 days after the study ends, unless a supervisor requires earlier deletion).

## 5. Benefits and risks

There is no direct clinical benefit. The photograph flash or room light may be briefly uncomfortable. Lid eversion may feel odd; you may stop at any time. There is a small privacy risk if a phone is lost; the on-phone database is encrypted.

## 6. Your rights

You may refuse or stop at any time. Stopping does not require a reason. If you stop before the operator saves the screening, nothing is stored. If you later want a server copy removed, tell the project lead **on the day of the visit** so they can note the anonymous session number on this form.
