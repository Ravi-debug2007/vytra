2. SLIDE 2 — PROPOSED SOLUTION
================================================================================

Official SIH heading: Proposed solution
  Must cover: detailed explanation / how it addresses the problem / uniqueness

THREE COLUMNS (or three stacked blocks):

A. THE PROBLEM
  - ASHA household visits have no lab and no hemoglobinometer.
  - Anemia in pregnancy is common; referral is still guesswork.
  - Visible jaundice is easy to miss in indoor light.
  - Families often never self-present at the PHC.
  - Extra gadgets and per-test fees die in the field.

B. WHAT VYTRA IS
  - Android app. Rear camera only. Zero consumables.
  - Photo 1 — everted lower lid (inner pink) → anemia risk.
  - Photo 2 — outer white of the open eye → jaundice risk.
  - Output: Low / Moderate / High / Unable to assess.
  - One referral PDF a PHC doctor can read.
  - Works in airplane mode. Telugu + English.

C. WHAT VYTRA IS NOT
  - Not a diagnosis.
  - Not a hemoglobin or bilirubin number.
  - Not a certified medical device.
  - Not a newborn / neonate tool in v1.
  - Not a cloud AI that needs 4G.
  - Not a consumer wellness selfie app.

UNIQUENESS STRIP (one line under the columns):
  Field protocol (pull the lid) + honest Unable-to-assess + Telugu-first UI
  + photo never saved + name only on the PDF.
  The "AI" is on-device colour maths — auditable, not a black box.

3. SLIDE 3 — TECHNICAL APPROACH
================================================================================

Official SIH heading: Technical approach
  Must cover: technologies + methodology / flowchart

TITLE LINE:
  On-device pipeline. No cloud inference. No extra hardware.

FLOW (8 steps, horizontal):
  1 Consent
  2 Visit note (skin-tone category + lighting)
  3 White paper (light calibration)
  4 Lid photos x2–3
  5 Sclera photos x2–3
  6 Median of valid shots
  7 Risk class
  8 Referral PDF

THREE CARDS:

STACK
  - Flutter / Dart  ·  Android 8+
  - Google ML Kit Face Mesh (aim only; Android-only Beta)
  - CIELAB colour maths on the phone (true Lab, not OpenCV uint8 Lab)
  - Encrypted SQLite (SQLCipher)  ·  local first
  - FastAPI sync  ·  optional, never required for a screening

SIGNAL
  - Anemia: conjunctiva a* (red–green)
  - Jaundice: sclera b* (yellow–blue)
  - At least 2 valid photos per series, then median
  - Fewer than 2 valid → UNABLE TO ASSESS (never fake Low)
  - Bins are prototype heuristics — not lab cutoffs

QUALITY GATES
  - Blur (Laplacian) + exposure
  - Eye-open check on sclera shots
  - White-paper calibration for room light
  - Static ellipse if Face Mesh drops (expected when the lid is pulled)
  - JPEG never written to disk

WHY THIS FITS A HACKATHON (2 lines):
  No custom optics, no model training, no 4G at the bedside.
  If time slips: mock cloud sync; cut Hindi, dashboards, neonate path.

DO NOT put a neural-net architecture diagram. There is no trained diagnostic net.

================================================================================

Official SIH heading: Feasibility and viability
  Must cover: feasibility + challenges/risks + how you overcome them

TITLE LINE:
  Six students. Fourteen days after nomination. Known risks, named cuts.

LEFT — WHY IT IS BUILDABLE
  - Hardware the ASHA already carries: her Android phone.
  - Colour maths is deterministic and unit-testable.
  - Spec pack already frozen (screens, schema, Telugu strings).
  - Demo path is airplane-mode complete. Venue Wi-Fi cannot kill it.
  - Team of 6: 2 Flutter, 1 vision, 1 backend, 1 design, 1 lead.
  - Sideloaded APK. No Play Store in the study window.
  - Minimum device: Android 8, ~8 MP rear camera, Snapdragon 665 class.

RIGHT — RISKS AND MITIGATION
  - Phone colour science differs
      → white-paper gains; log device model; no cross-phone accuracy claim.
  - Pulling the eyelid breaks Face Mesh
      → static ellipse is the anemia path. Designed, not a bug.
  - Newborn faces fail the mesh
      → neonates explicitly out of v1.
  - Telugu PDF may render as boxes
      → Day-1 font-embed go/no-go.
  - Mesh under 20 FPS on a cheap SoC
      → drop overlay, keep quality gates.
  - Ethics / consent
      → paper form + in-app gate; faculty signs copy.

BOTTOM — IF TIME SLIPS (three chips)
  KEEP:  capture, quality gates, CIELAB class, disclaimer, local save, PDF, Telugu

9. Q&A LINES THE DECK MUST SUPPORT (do not put all of these on slides)
================================================================================

Q: Is this a medical device?
A: No. Triage aid. Disclaimer on every result and every PDF. PHC still does the blood test.

Q: Can it tell hemoglobin?
A: No. Never displays Hb or bilirubin. Only Low / Moderate / High / Unable to assess.

Q: What is the accuracy?
A: We will not claim a clinical accuracy number. Thresholds are prototype heuristics.

Q: Didn't Tamir already do this in 2017?
A: Tamir used RGB R-vs-G on 19 people. Prior art. Our product is the ASHA workflow.

Q: Why not a deep-learning model?
A: A 14-day team cannot train a reliable on-device segmenter. Deterministic colour we can defend.

Q: Newborn jaundice is the emergency. Why cut neonates?
A: Face Mesh fails on newborn faces. Shipping kernicterus copy with a camera that cannot lock a face is unsafe. v2.

Q: How do you photograph the inner eyelid?
A: Look up, gently pull the lower lid, fill the ring with inner pink. Mesh often drops. Ellipse still works.

Q: Internet?
A: Core flow is airplane-mode complete, including the PDF.
